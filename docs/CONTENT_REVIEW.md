# Clinical content review

Use this process for learner-facing clinical or AI-safety content.

## Required metadata

Each substantive module should record:

- title and learning objectives;
- intended audience and assumed background;
- author and reviewer roles;
- sources and access dates;
- last reviewed date;
- known limitations and planned re-review date.

## Review checklist

- Clinical statements match the cited sources.
- Sources are authoritative and sufficiently current for the claim.
- Examples use synthetic or properly de-identified information.
- AI output is labeled and not presented as authoritative.
- Uncertainty, failure modes, bias, and limitations are visible to learners.
- The material does not provide patient-specific medical advice.
- Accessibility and plain-language needs have been considered.
- Assessments measure the stated learning objectives.

## Change control

Changes to clinical claims, learner assessment, or safety language should be reviewed by someone with appropriate subject-matter expertise before merge. Record that review in the pull request.
