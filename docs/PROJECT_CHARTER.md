# Project charter

## Purpose

The Clinical AI Education Tool is intended to support learning about the responsible use, evaluation, and limitations of AI in clinical contexts.

## Current phase

The project is establishing its scope, educational content, technical architecture, and review process. The repository should not yet be presented as a validated or production-ready application.

## Guiding principles

1. **Education before automation:** Help learners reason about AI rather than defer to it.
2. **Human accountability:** Qualified people remain responsible for clinical and educational decisions.
3. **Evidence and transparency:** Cite claims, disclose uncertainty, and document material design decisions.
4. **Privacy by design:** Use synthetic or properly de-identified data and minimize collection.
5. **Equity and accessibility:** Evaluate differential impacts and design for inclusive access.
6. **Continuous review:** Reassess content as evidence, models, and guidance change.

## Out of scope unless formally reviewed

- Diagnosis, triage, treatment, or patient-specific advice
- Autonomous clinical decision-making
- Storage of patient records or sensitive learner data
- Claims of clinical validation, regulatory status, or institutional endorsement

## Near-term decisions

- Define primary learners and learning objectives.
- Select the application architecture and hosting model.
- Choose a license and contribution terms.
- Establish named clinical, educational, privacy, and technical reviewers.
- Define evaluation criteria before public release.
