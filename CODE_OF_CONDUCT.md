# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, profession, or level of seniority.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Welcome questions and constructive disagreement.
- Protect patient, learner, and contributor privacy.
- Give credit for ideas and work.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disclosure of private information, or sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
